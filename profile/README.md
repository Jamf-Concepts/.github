## What is Jamf Concepts?

Jamf Concepts is our innovation lab, where employees and teams from across Jamf come together to build tools, apps, and integrations that address additional and emerging customer outcomes. These aren't official products, although some of them might be one day. They're built by people who are passionate and close to the problems that need solving.

Concepts move fast. They're meant to explore and experiment. Many are used by the Jamf community across test and production environments. Some grow to be included as official Jamf capabilities. Others remain as open-source tools or community resources. All of them benefit from your feedback.

### What You'll Find Here

Concepts come in many forms: native apps for Apple platforms, web apps, utilities, integrations, and open-source projects. Whatever the form, they all start with someone at Jamf who had the passion to build it for our customers and community.

## concepts.jamf.com

[concepts.jamf.com](https://concepts.jamf.com) features a curated selection of the most broadly applicable Concepts — the ones with the widest reach, the most complete documentation, and full solution guides to help you get started. If something is on the site, it's been through additional review and write-up to make it as approachable as possible.

This GitHub org is the full picture. Every Concept that has been made publicly available lives here, including work that is newer, more specialized, or still finding its audience. Not every repo on GitHub has a page on the site yet — that doesn't reflect the quality of the work, just where it is in its journey.

If you're exploring what exists, start here. If you're trying to implement something, the solution guides at [concepts.jamf.com/guides](https://concepts.jamf.com/guides) are the best place to go deep.
